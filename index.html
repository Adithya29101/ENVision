A GREAT CIVILIZATION IS NOT CONQUERED FROM WITHOUT UNTIL IT HAS DESTROYED ITSELF FROM WITHIN

Set in the final days of the ancient Maya empire, Apocalypto delivers a gripping cinematic journey of survival, endurance, and courage through the Mesoamerican rainforest.

Cinematic Themes

The philosophical and historical underpinnings of the story

Love & Protection

Driven by unwavering devotion, Jaguar Paw overcomes extraordinary hazards to return and rescue his trapped family. Illustrates how political corruption, environmental exhaustion, and fear undermine societies from within long before external invasion.

Behind The Scenes

Authentic craftsmanship, location shooting, and language preservation

Veracruz Rainforests

Filmed extensively in the Catemaco rainforests of Mexico, utilizing authentic natural settings for realism.

Yucatec Maya Dialogue

Spoken completely in Yucatec Maya to provide complete historical immersion and cultural resonance.

Indigenous Cast

Features an ensemble cast composed almost entirely of Native American and Indigenous Mexican actors.
